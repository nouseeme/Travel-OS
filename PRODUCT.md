## Platform

## Product Purpose

An invite-only workspace for Egimu Travel connecting sales enquiries, trip operations, personal tasks, internal documents, contacts, team access, and cash records. Product capabilities and terminology below are recorded from README.md and the incumbent application, not new product decisions.

## Users

The existing application serves travel sales/PICs, trip operations, finance, administrators, and scoped external trip collaborators. These are responsibilities rather than fixed departments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The October 26, 2026 user request authorizes a complete visual redesign and polish, including mobile. Preserve existing routes, navigation destinations, workflows, actions, data, financial semantics, permissions, forms, and error recovery. Any proposed flow/UX change requires a clearly identified flag and explicit approval before implementation. Work is limited to presentation; backend and data model changes are outside scope.

## Brand Commitments

Keep travelOS and Egimu Travel identity. The user requests clean, modern, easy-to-skim, readable interfaces with useful information. The six supplied images guide visual styling only, not dashboard structure. They show restrained neutral surfaces, readable hierarchy, quiet outlines, limited pastel status colors, and generous but practical spacing.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, existing routes and components, public/brand/egimu-logo.jpg, and the user's six attached inspiration images. Existing UI copy and real data remain authoritative. Preview data is isolated from the workspace and must not ship as product content.

## Product Principles

- Make existing information and actions easy to find.
- Preserve operational and financial correctness.
- Keep the same capabilities usable on narrow screens.
- Use visual hierarchy to distinguish content, metadata, and state.
